Who We Are

A family built on grace, growing in purpose.

Since our founding in 2024, Christ Apostolic Church, Christ Alone Prayer Mountain (Finger of God DCC) has been a spiritual home for individuals and families in Ilaro, Ogun State — offering sound biblical teaching, fervent prayer, and warm community.

Led by Pastor Olusegun Oladeji (H.O.J. Idunnu Jesu), we exist to help people encounter God, discover their purpose, and live it out with courage.
