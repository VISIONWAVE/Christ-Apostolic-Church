Our Story

About Christ Apostolic Church

A family built on prayer and the Word, growing together in purpose since 2024.

Our History

Two years of God's faithfulness

Christ Apostolic Church, Christ Alone Prayer Mountain (Finger of God DCC) was founded in 2024 in Ilaro, Ogun State, out of a burden for fervent, Spirit-led prayer and sound biblical teaching. In just two years, by God's grace, we have grown into a family devoted to prayer, prophetic ministry, and discipleship.

Today we gather for Sunday School and our Glorious Service, hold weekly Prophetic Bible Study and Revival Hour meetings, and run monthly vigils and prophetic gatherings for our growing community in Ilaro and beyond.

Our Vision

To raise a generation anchored in grace, equipped to live out their God-given purpose in every sphere of life.

Our Mission

To preach the gospel with clarity, disciple believers with intentionality, and serve our city with compassion.

Senior Pastor

Pastor Olusegun Oladeji (H.O.J. Idunnu Jesu)

Popularly known as H.O.J. Idunnu Jesu, Pastor Olusegun Oladeji has served as Minister in Charge of Christ Apostolic Church, Christ Alone Prayer Mountain (Finger of God DCC) since its founding in 2024. He is known for a strong prophetic and prayer-focused ministry, calling the church to encounter God through fervent prayer and the sound teaching of His Word.

He leads a growing ministry team — including Pst Ayodeji Onigbogi (Assistant Pastor) — and regularly partners with visiting ministers and evangelists to strengthen the church's prophetic and revival culture.

— Pastor Olusegun Oladeji (H.O.J. Idunnu Jesu)

What We Believe

Our Core Beliefs

01

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our final authority for faith and life.

02

The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

03

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is a free gift received through faith in Jesus Christ, not by works.

04

The Church

We believe the local church is God's instrument for discipleship, worship, and service to the world.

05

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit empowers believers to live godly lives and to serve with spiritual gifts.

06

Eternity

We believe in the bodily resurrection of the dead and eternal life for all who trust in Christ.
